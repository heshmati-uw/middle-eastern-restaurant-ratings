# All three core-tag precinct comparisons

Download [core_precinct_summary.zip](core_precinct_summary.zip), extract, and open analysis_clean/precinct_core_summary/combined_report.html. One table contains all three comparisons, separately by Trump/non-Trump county and election/ban, with raw p-values, group-specific restaurant Ns, total Ns, county counts, and confidence intervals. Exact state coverage and restaurant counts by state/group/event are included.

Interpretation: first-group-minus-second-group rating-change gradient per +10 percentage points of precinct Trump share. Core tags only; four-week windows; equal restaurant weights; county fixed effects; county-clustered inference. These are gradients in local political support, not average event effects.

The union includes 43 states plus DC. Election samples span 42 states plus DC; ban samples 43 plus DC. Iowa appears only in the ban samples. Across these models, the excluded states are Alaska, Mississippi, Montana, North Dakota, South Dakota, Vermont, and Wyoming. Each pair/stratum has its own common-county sample, so state lists differ by row; this coverage does not describe the entire raw-data universe.
