Restaurant ratings around the travel ban

ME and WPME in Trump-majority and non-Trump-majority counties

FiguresRegressionsDescriptivesCorrelationsMethods

In the six-week restaurant-level model with eight weeks of historical controls, WPME gained 0.213 stars relative to ME in Trump-majority counties (p = .028). The corresponding change was −0.004 stars elsewhere (p = .926). The direct county-type difference was +0.216 stars (95% CI 0.024 to 0.409, p = .028).

Methods

  • Groups. ME follows the original primary-category rules. WPME is primary Mediterranean with a hummus or baba ghanoush mention in pre-election reviews.
  • Restaurant model. Average all rated raw reviews in each period, then regress each restaurant's post-minus-pre rating change on positioning, controls and county fixed effects. Each restaurant has equal weight. The common sample contains 2,346 ME and WPME restaurants observed under both aligned window definitions, in counties containing both groups.
  • Dates. The main pre-period is December 16, 2016–January 26, 2017. The post-period is January 27–March 9, 2017. The four-week check uses December 30–January 26 and January 27–February 23. Both use January 27 as the calendar-date boundary in UTC.
  • Controls. The eight-week history pools October 10–November 6 and November 14–December 11. It supplies the mean rating and log(1 + review count), with a missing-rating indicator. The four-week history uses the first block. Models add price tier, listed dine-in, takeout, delivery and alcohol, and indicators for missing records. Price and service metadata are undated.
  • Weekly model. Friday–Thursday bins are plotted at Monday midpoints, with the January 27 marker. The plotted model uses the original four-week historical controls alongside price and service measures interacted with week. Each restaurant receives equal total weight across its observed weeks. The bars show pointwise 95% confidence intervals clustered by county. The weekly sample requires two observed pre bins and one post bin in the original paired-ban cohort.
  • Interpretation. The restaurant and weekly estimates are correlational. Earlier pretrend diagnostics varied by window and specification.
