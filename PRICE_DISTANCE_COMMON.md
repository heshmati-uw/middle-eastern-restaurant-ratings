# Common samples: price tier and geographic preference

Exact dollar-sign price matching, geographic-distance preference, no same-county requirement and no distance cap. Trump and non-Trump county strata are matched separately. Each triplet contains one ME, one WPME and one MED; all three comparisons use the same restaurants. Labels use full raw pre-election core review tags. Holidays included. Price metadata are undated.

Extract price_distance_common.zip into the repository/workspace root and open analysis_clean/full_pre_election_core/attribute_matching/price_distance_common/common_sample_report.html.

N per group: election Trump 84, non-Trump 642; ban Trump 87, non-Trump 698. Four-week rating-change comparisons give equal weight to restaurants. None of the 12 contrasts has raw p<.10. Ban Trump ME-WPME=-.114 stars (p=.428); non-Trump +.072 (p=.229). The larger overlap sample changes the target population.

Geographic tradeoff: median distance among all three pair contrasts in ban Trump triplets is 196 km; ME-WPME median is 270 km. Some matches span states and thousands of km. The algorithm optimizes distance from the smallest group in each price stratum; it is not a globally optimal three-way distance assignment. See design.json and distance_summary.csv.

Mean model retains triplet FE and county-clustered CR1 variance; because triplets can now cross counties, their fixed effects are nonnested in clusters and the finite-sample correction accounts for this. Weekly models retain restaurant and county-by-week FE and equal total restaurant weights, with a common three-group eligible cohort. Full pretrend p for ban Trump ME-WPME=.00614 (not reassuring); non-Trump=.370 (non-rejection does not prove parallel trends). Undated attributes and wide geographic matches limit causal interpretation.

Reproduce from workspace root:
python analysis_clean/price_distance_common_matching.py
Rscript analysis_clean/common_triplet_models.R --cross-county
python analysis_clean/report_common_triplets.py --cross-county

Requires Python numpy/pandas/scipy and R data.table/fixest/jsonlite. The matching script expects scipy in .analysis-python or the Python environment. Frozen inputs are included. Large verification input CSVs are regenerated by the R script. Independent checks verified all 12 mean contrasts and four weekly models, including county-clustered covariance matrices, common sample identities and equal total restaurant weights. No reviewer text or reviewer IDs are included.
