Is ME’s decline larger in Trump-majority counties?

In the four-week election window, yes in the unadjusted direct test: ME’s change relative to American restaurants is 0.217 stars more negative in Trump-majority counties (two-sided p = 0.031). The corresponding ban-period difference is not statistically clear (−0.122 stars; p = 0.128).

The election p-value becomes 0.063 after Holm adjustment across the two requested ME–American shock tests. The four-week window was chosen after inspecting the earlier window results, so these remain exploratory findings.

Same windows and equal restaurant weights

  • Election: October 10–November 6, 2016 versus November 14–December 11, 2016.
  • Ban: December 26, 2016–January 22, 2017 versus January 30–February 26, 2017.

The complete shock week is excluded. Every restaurant must have reviews in both periods. Reviews are averaged within each restaurant-period first, then each restaurant’s change receives equal weight. All groups use outcomes from the same rebuilt combined weekly source.

Trump-majority counties have Republican vote share greater than 50%; other counties have share at or below 50%. “Non-Trump” is this operational threshold, not necessarily a Democratic-majority or Democratic-winner definition. Missing county/vote shares are excluded.

Estimation and verification

Within each county-vote group, OLS regresses each restaurant’s after-minus-before mean rating on a cuisine-group indicator. The direct test pools the two county-vote groups and adds the county-vote indicator and its interaction with the cuisine indicator. The interaction coefficient equals the difference between subgroup contrasts. In period-level notation, it is the post × cuisine × Trump-county interaction.

Standard errors cluster by county, using G/(G−1) × (N−1)/(N−K) small-sample correction; two-sided t tests and intervals use G−1 degrees of freedom. A directional, negative-tail p-value is also exported for the direct test, but the headline results use two-sided inference.

All 13,861 candidate IDs were found in the source file. The control pool is constructed using the same historical primary-American eligibility and geocoding rules in both county groups, rather than using keyword-selected American restaurants in one group. Focal ME/WPME classifications use the same modern mapping in both groups. Adding the non-Trump data reproduces all six prior Trump-county coefficients, standard errors and p-values exactly.

Independent NumPy regressions and county sandwich calculations reproduce all 18 model estimates/standard errors. Restaurant-period means were independently reconstructed as well. These are descriptive group-change contrasts, not causal effects of the election/ban or proof of discrimination. County types can differ in restaurant/reviewer composition, seasonality and local conditions; no county-fixed-effect or demographic adjustment is imposed in these raw-mean contrasts.
